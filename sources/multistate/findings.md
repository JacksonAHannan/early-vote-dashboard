# Additional states: source and comparison contract

The dashboard extends the Texas workflow with state-native county observations. Source-level facts and collection verification are retained independently:

- [Georgia](ga/findings.md): SOS-linked Qlik aggregates;159counties, race/age/sex, accepted-date chronology and certified2024county presidential results. No residence precinct or registration party is exposed by the source.
- [North Carolina](nc/findings.md): NCSBE accepted ballot records processed only in memory;100counties plus current-only precinct counts. Actual voter demographics and registration party are distinct from ecological Census composition. Five historical and one current in-person control differences reflect independently published files, not a reason to erase counties.
- [Virginia](va/findings.md): EPEC publishes anonymous aggregates of the official ELECT Daily Absentee List. This is independently republished source transport, not direct ELECT download or certified final turnout.133localities have current county history. The available precinct archive stopsSeptember23; it is never carried forward toOctober23. Native aliases and67county/precinct reconciliation differences are retained, not assigned to particular precincts.
- [Nevada](nv/findings.md): official Clark and Washoe records. Clark supports dated2024comparisons, Washoe currently supports2026reported totals. Returned mail includes pending processing/signature resolution and excludes undeliverable/canceled/surrendered records. Ballot typeNA is not registration party. A152vote Clark site/roster difference is disclosed; its cause is unresolved and votes are not allocated to precincts.

## Calendar and membership

Cross-cycle dates have the same number of days untilElectionDay, November5,2024 andNovember3,2026. October23,2026 therefore pairs withOctober25,2024. State-specific opening dates and future historical benchmarks remain visible. Daily and cumulative membership are resolved separately for every county, voting method and voter-group point. Exact published snapshots take precedence; missing observations and negative cumulative differences are unavailable, not synthetic zeros. Current county totals without historical counterparts are inspectable as **reported**, while comparisons use only paired members.

Voter-group categories require a known category count in both years; collectors materialize confirmed source zeros only within published date coverage. Unknown racial/ethnic classifications stay separate from affirmative non-white classifications. Registration-party groups do not identify candidate choice. None of these early-vote figures establishes final turnout or a causal demographic effect.

The current-only precinct inspector does not imply a boundary correspondence across elections. Exact cumulative precinct snapshots do not mix old alias daily histories into newly published snapshots, and the absence of a selected-date publication does not carry old precinct counts forward. County headline figures always come from the county observations, not unverified precinct rollups.

## County composition and maps

The source is the Census Bureau's [2020–2024ACS CVAP special tabulation](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/decennial-census/about/voting-rights/cvap/2020-2024-CVAP.html), specifically **ADU_EST** and **TOT_EST** in its [County.csv archive](https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/decennial/rdo/datasets/2024/2024-cvap/CVAP_2020-2024_ACS_csv_files.zip). Adult estimates include non-citizens; they are not CVAP counts. White non-Hispanic uses line7; total non-white is line1adult minus line7adult. Hispanic uses line13, Black non-Hispanic alone line5, Asian non-Hispanic alone line4. Under18 is total population minus adults, divided by total population. These are survey estimates of residents, not voter records. The [technical documentation](https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/decennial/rdo/technical-documentation/special-tabulation/CVAP_2020-2024_ACS_documentation_v1.pdf) describes definitions, rounding and sampling uncertainty.

The Census [county boundary service](https://tigerweb.geo.census.gov/arcgis/rest/services/Census2020/tigerWMS_Census2020/MapServer/82) supplies409county/city features:159GA,100NC,133VA,17NV. StableFIPS joins are checked against all four Census tables. Geometry is simplified for county display; no votes are interpolated. Raw public aggregate inputs and their SHA256 fingerprints are retained in `reference/`; per-state reference/map outputs are under `dashboard/data/states/`. Rebuild with `python3 dashboard/scripts/build_multistate_reference.py --offline`.

VA presidential classifications use the official [2024presidential contest](https://historical.elections.virginia.gov/contest/161256) and its linked [results CSV](https://va2.elstats.civera.com/api/download_contest/161256_table.csv?split_party=false). All133locality rows sum to2,335,395Harris and2,075,085Trump, matching its separate statewide row. The other states' official return provenance is in their state findings.
